# ESCC Student Project Ideas & Outreach Activities

Brainstormed project ideas for Eastern Shore Community College undergraduate students and outreach/education activities aligned with the EI Climate Collaborative ESVA Septic Project (2026-2028).

---

## Student Research Projects

Organized by project research thrust. Projects are scoped for ESCC capstone courses, internships, or NSF REU-style engagements (typically one semester or one summer).

### Thrust 1: Sensor Monitoring & Early Warning

| # | Project | PI Alignment | What Students Would Do |
|---|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Field Monitoring Technician Program** | Majid, Teresa | Assist Green Stream with sensor installation, maintenance, and data collection at pilot households. Builds technical skills and reduces labor costs. |
| 2 | **Sensor Data Quality & Validation** | Majid, Teresa | Compare sensor readings (groundwater level, soil moisture) against manual measurements on regular site visits. Intro to data science and QA/QC. |
| 3 | **Weather-to-Flush-Failure Diary Study** | Allison, Molly | Recruit households to keep simple logs correlating rain events with septic symptoms (backup, odor, standing water). Creates qualitative dataset to complement sensors. |

### Thrust 2: Decision Support & Community Tools

| # | Project | PI Alignment | What Students Would Do |
|---|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| 4 | **Septic System Decision Game** *(existing idea)* | Majid, Barbara | Design a board game or app prototype walking homeowners through septic maintenance decisions and costs. Pilot at Earth Day or SepticSmart Month events. |
| 5 | **StoryMap Expansion / Local Knowledge Mapping** | Molly, Barbara | Interview residents in Parksley, Exmore, Nassawadox to document local drainage knowledge -- flooding spots, important ditches, historical problem areas. Ground-truth for models. |
| 6 | **Cost-of-Inaction Calculator** | Allison, Majid | Build a simple spreadsheet or web tool showing homeowners the financial consequences of delaying septic maintenance vs. regular pump-out costs. Supports "pump-out literacy" priority. |

### Thrust 3: Data & Public Health Groundwork

| # | Project | PI Alignment | What Students Would Do |
|---|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| 7 | **Septic & Well Record Digitization** | Teresa, Molly | Digitize and geocode existing paper septic/well records into the project's geospatial database. Core Thrust 3 work well-suited to trained student labor. |
| 8 | **Well Water Sampling at Community Events** | Teresa, Alex F | Collect and test well water samples at Earth Day or other events, giving residents immediate results while building a pilot contamination dataset. |
| 9 | **Historical Failure Pattern Analysis** | Allison, Molly | Map VDH's ~90 repairs/year (with tax map numbers) against the 2040 groundwater depth predictions to test whether high-risk zones match actual failure patterns. |

### Thrust 4: Equitable Financing & Community Engagement

| # | Project | PI Alignment | What Students Would Do |
|---|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| 10 | **Septic Assistance Program Inventory** | Allison, Barbara | Research and compile a comprehensive guide to every available grant, cost-share, and assistance program for septic repair/replacement on the Shore. Directly useful to residents. |

| 12 | **"What's in Your Septic?" Household Survey** | Barbara, Alex F | Conduct door-to-door or event-based surveys on septic maintenance habits, awareness of pump-out programs, and barriers to action. Feeds education strategy and equity analysis. |

## Notes

- All outreach should emphasize the **"educate and notify, not enforce"** approach agreed upon with VDH
- Materials should be accessible and avoid creating anxiety about property values or enforcement risk (per VDH's "do no harm" concerns)
- Spanish-language versions of key materials should be developed where feasible
- Student co-authorship on articles and presentations builds their portfolios and deepens community trust
